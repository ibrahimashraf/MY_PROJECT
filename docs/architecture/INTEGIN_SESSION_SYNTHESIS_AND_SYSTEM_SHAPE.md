## 3. Deep Physical Mechanics & The Rigging Train (Domain 2)

Heavy lifting fails when software assumes clean, linear textbook conditions. INTEGIN’s mechanics engine directly models the true non-linear reality:

### A. The Indeterminate Ground Contact & Outrigger Solver
- **The Flat-Plane Fallacy**: A 4-point outrigger crane chassis is not an infinitely rigid plane. On non-homogeneous soil ($k_1 \ne k_2 \ne k_3 \ne k_4$), four points of support are statically indeterminate.
- **Iterative Contact Relaxation (Lift-Off Detection)**:
  - If calculated pad reaction $P_i \le 0$, the pad has lost contact with the ground ($P_i = 0$).
  - The solver drops that constraint and re-equilibrates forces diagonally across the remaining active outriggers.
  - Detects impending **diagonal rocking and soil punch-through failure** before physical tipping occurs.

### B. The Complete Rigging Load Train
Beyond shackles, the engine models every element of the mechanical chain:
1. **Padeyes & Trunnions (ASME BTH-1 / AISC 360)**: Pin-bearing stress ($\sigma_b = P / (d \cdot t)$), hole tear-out, cheek plate weld shear, and out-of-plane transverse bending.
2. **Spreader Beams & Modular Trusses (DIN EN 13155)**: Euler-Johnson column buckling under axial compression combined with self-weight bending.
3. **Slings & Grommets (IMCA M 179 / DNV-ST-N001)**: Cable-laid grommet efficiency deratings, high-modulus synthetic (HMPE/Dyneema) creep elongation, thermal cutoffs ($>65^\circ\text{C}$), and choke angle deratings ($\alpha < 120^\circ$).
4. **Reeving & Sheaves (DIN 15020)**: Groove contact pressure ($p = 2S / (D \cdot d)$), fleet angle geometric limits ($\le 1.5^\circ$ smooth, $\le 2.0^\circ$ grooved), and multi-part line friction efficiency (Eytelwein capstan equation).
5. **Hooks (DIN 15401 / DIN 15402)**: Curved beam Winkler-Bach flexural stress distribution across single and duplex (ramshorn) hook cross-sections.
6. **Transport & Base Supports**: SPMT 3-point vs. 4-point hydraulic suspension stability, tipping angles, and heavy timber crane mat punch shear.

### C. Coupled Multi-Environment Continuum
- **Atmospheric Boundary Layer**: ASCE 7 / Eurocode 1 power-law velocity profiles ($v(z) = v_{\text{ref}}(z/z_0)^\alpha$) driving aerodynamic drag and vortex-induced vibration (VIV).
